Privacy Policy — Sup Poker

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Sup Poker (“the app”) is developed by Sup Digital (“we”).

Short version

  • The Odds tab works entirely on your phone. The cards you enter and the results are never stored or sent.
  • The Tournaments tab shows a list of festivals built into the app, and poker news headlines that your phone fetches directly from the news sites in your source list. Those sites see the request, as with any website visit. Nothing else is sent.
  • Local games is optional. If you use it, the details you choose to share (a name, a contact, your country and area, the games you post and the messages you write) go to the Sup Poker rooms service, which we run on Google Apps Script with the records kept in a Google Sheet. Your contact is shown only to players you accept, and only while your game is open.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking. There is no account, email address or password.
  • You can delete your Local games data from the app at any time.

The Odds tab

Equity, outs, the nuts and pot odds are all worked out on your phone. Nothing you enter there is stored or sent anywhere.

The Tournaments tab

  • Festival list. It is part of the app. Each entry links to the organiser’s official page, which opens in your browser only when you tap it.
  • News. When you open the tab (at most once every 30 minutes, or whenever you tap refresh), your phone downloads the news feeds of the sites in your source list. By default these are PokerNews, Somuchpoker, CardPlayer, PokerTube and Pokerfuse; you can remove any of them and add others. The app sends those sites nothing but the request itself. Like any website, they see your IP address and the app’s browser identifier, under their own privacy policies.
  • The headlines are kept on your phone so the tab still works offline; tapping one opens the story in your browser. Your source list stays on your phone.

Local games (optional)

What is sent and stored when you use it

  • An identity your phone creates: a random user id and a secret. The secret stays on your phone; the service keeps only its SHA-256 hash. A new phone is a new user.
  • Your profile: the name you choose, a contact (for example a Telegram handle), and your country and area: a region, or the city or district you type. Never your address or your phone’s location.
  • Games you post: the time, area, game and format, blinds, seats, whether there is a buy-in, and your note.
  • Requests to join a game: your name, the message you write, and the request’s status.
  • Reports you make: the reason you give, and your user id.
  • When each of these was created or changed.

Your phone’s country setting is read once, on the phone, to pre-select your country.

Who sees what

  • Anyone using the app can see listed games with the host’s chosen name, the area and the game, never the host’s contact or id.
  • A host sees the name and message of each player who asks to join their game.
  • A host’s contact is shown only to the players that host accepted, and only while the game is open.
  • We can see everything stored in the service, to run it and to moderate it, for example to take down a reported game.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • The rooms service is a Google Apps Script web app and a Google Sheet in our Google account. Google hosts them under its own terms and privacy policy. Everything travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • A game is listed until 6 hours after its start. The records stay in the sheet until you delete them or ask us to.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: in Local games, open your profile and tap Delete my data. This erases your name, contact, messages and notes from every game, request and report you made, closes your open games, withdraws your requests, and removes your identity from the phone.
  • By email: write to the address below and we will do the same for you.
  • You can use the Odds and Tournaments tabs without ever opening Local games.

Android permissions

Internet only, for the news headlines and Local games. The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. Android’s backup is turned off for this app, so your Local games identity is never copied to another device.

Children

Sup Poker is for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above changes too, and material changes are noted in the app’s Play Store release notes.
