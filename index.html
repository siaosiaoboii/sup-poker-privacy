Privacy Policy — Sup Poker

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Sup Poker (“the app”) is developed by Sup Digital (“we”).

Short version

  • We receive no data from the app. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours, and it sends us nothing.
  • The Odds tab works entirely on your phone. The cards you enter, the cards the camera reads and the results are never stored or sent.
  • The camera is used only if you switch on the Odds tab’s camera mode, to read the cards on the table. Each picture is read on your phone and discarded at once; no picture is ever saved or sent.
  • The Tournaments tab shows a list of festivals built into the app, and poker news headlines that your phone fetches directly from the news sites in your source list. Those sites see the request, as with any website visit. Nothing else is sent.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • A host can stream a drawn table of their game to their own Twitch or YouTube channel: seats, cards on the table, bets, pot and a computer voice, never a camera picture, a microphone or a name (see Table stream).
  • Follow your local laws, and look after yourself. Poker for money is regulated differently everywhere; it is your responsibility to know and follow the law where you are, and to use your own judgement and care at any game. The app is provided as it is, and we accept no liability for how it is used or misused (see Your local laws and Your responsibility and our liability).

The Odds tab

Equity, outs, the nuts and pot odds are all worked out on your phone. Nothing you enter there is stored or sent anywhere.

Camera mode

If you switch on the camera mode in the Odds tab, the app asks for camera access the first time, then uses your phone’s back camera to read the community cards (the flop, turn and river) on the table in front of it and fill them in. It never reads your own cards: you enter those yourself.

  • Each picture is analysed on your phone the moment it is taken and then discarded. No picture or video is ever saved, shown to anyone else or sent anywhere, and nothing the camera reads leaves your phone.
  • The camera runs only while the camera mode is on and the Odds tab is open. It stops when you switch the mode off, go to another tab or leave the app.
  • You can withdraw camera access at any time in your phone’s settings. Everything else in the app works without it.

The Tournaments tab

  • Festival list. It is part of the app. Each entry links to the organiser’s official page, which opens in your browser only when you tap it.
  • News. When you open the tab (at most once every 30 minutes, or whenever you tap refresh), your phone downloads the news feeds of the sites in your source list. By default these are PokerNews, Somuchpoker, CardPlayer, PokerTube and Pokerfuse; you can remove any of them and add others. The app sends those sites nothing but the request itself. Like any website, they see your IP address and the app’s browser identifier, under their own privacy policies.
  • The headlines are kept on your phone so the tab still works offline; tapping one opens the story in your browser. Your source list stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your news source list and the last headlines fetched are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, where only the app can read them. Uninstalling the app removes them. Android’s backup is turned off for this app, so they are never copied to another device. Pictures from the camera are not kept at all. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Table stream (hosts of Local games)

A host can stream their game to their own Twitch or YouTube channel from the app. What is streamed is a drawing, made on the phone: a table with numbered seats (or nicknames the host types), the community cards, the bets and the pot, and a computer voice reading the action. No picture from the camera, no sound from the microphone and no player’s name, face or voice is ever in it. A player’s cards appear only when they turn them face up at the table. The stream can be delayed by three minutes.

  • The stream goes from the phone straight to Twitch or YouTube under the host’s own account and their terms; we receive nothing. The host’s stream key stays in the app’s private storage on the phone and is used only to send the stream.
  • The camera, if the host switches it on, reads only the community cards on the table, as in the Odds tab. The voice is the phone’s own text-to-speech.
  • It is the host’s responsibility to tell the players that the game is streamed, and to follow the rules of Twitch or YouTube and the law where they are.

Local games

This version of Sup Poker has no Local games (game rooms shared with other players). If a later version adds them, this policy will say what they send and store before that version is released.

Android permissions

Internet, for the news headlines, and the camera, for the Odds tab’s camera mode only (asked for the first time you switch it on). The app does not use your microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

Sup Poker is for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children.

It does not arrange, host, run or take part in any game or wager, handles no money, and gives no legal advice. Check the rules where you live before you play for money.
