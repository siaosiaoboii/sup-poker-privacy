Privacy Policy — Sup Poker

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Sup Poker (“the app”) is developed by Sup Digital (“we”).

Short version

  • We receive no data from the app. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours, and it sends us nothing.
  • The Odds tab works entirely on your phone. The cards you enter and the results are never stored or sent. If you turn on card reading, the camera pictures are analysed on your phone and never stored or sent either.
  • The Tournaments tab shows a list of festivals built into the app, and poker news headlines that your phone fetches directly from the news sites in your source list. Those sites see the request, as with any website visit. Nothing else is sent.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.

The Odds tab

Equity, outs, the nuts and pot odds are all worked out on your phone. Nothing you enter there is stored or sent anywhere.

Card reading (optional). You can let the app read the community cards (the flop, turn and river) through the phone’s camera held over the table. The camera is used only while you switch card reading on, the pictures are analysed on your phone and are never stored or sent, and the app reads only the cards on the table, never a player’s hand. Every other feature works without granting the camera permission.

The Tournaments tab

  • Festival list. It is part of the app. Each entry links to the organiser’s official page, which opens in your browser only when you tap it.
  • News. When you open the tab (at most once every 30 minutes, or whenever you tap refresh), your phone downloads the news feeds of the sites in your source list. By default these are PokerNews, Somuchpoker, CardPlayer, PokerTube and Pokerfuse; you can remove any of them and add others. The app sends those sites nothing but the request itself. Like any website, they see your IP address and the app’s browser identifier, under their own privacy policies.
  • The headlines are kept on your phone so the tab still works offline; tapping one opens the story in your browser. Your source list stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your news source list and the last headlines fetched are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, where only the app can read them. Uninstalling the app removes them. Android’s backup is turned off for this app, so they are never copied to another device. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Local games

This version of Sup Poker has no Local games (game rooms shared with other players). If a later version adds them, this policy will say what they send and store before that version is released.

Android permissions

Internet, for the news headlines, and Camera only if you turn on card reading in the Odds tab (see above). The app does not use your microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

Sup Poker is for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above changes too, and material changes are noted in the app’s Play Store release notes.
